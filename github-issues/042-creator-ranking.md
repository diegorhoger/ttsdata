### User story

As a user, I want to discover creators in my niche to learn from their content strategies.

### Requirements

- Rank creators by follower count, engagement rate, video count
- Show creator's top-performing videos
- Show creator's product categories
- Calculate creator "influence score" (followers × engagement)
- Support search by creator name, category, niche

### Acceptance criteria

- [ ] 1000+ creators ranked and searchable
- [ ] Creator detail page with videos and products
